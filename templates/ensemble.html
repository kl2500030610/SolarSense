2. Feature Selection & Leakage Prevention

Included Features

  • IRRADIATION — Solar irradiation (W/m²). Primary driver of power generation; directly determines panel output.
  • MODULE_TEMPERATURE — Panel surface temperature (°C). Affects cell efficiency; strongly correlated with irradiation.
  • AMBIENT_TEMPERATURE — Air temperature (°C). Influences module temperature and overall plant performance.
  • PLANT_ID — Which of the two solar plants (one-hot encoded). Accounts for systematic differences in plant design.

Dropped Columns (Leakage Avoidance)

  • DC_POWER — Near-perfect correlate of AC_POWER within each plant. Using it would leak the target.
  • DAILY_YIELD — Daily accumulated AC_POWER. Derived from the target, not available at prediction time.
  • TOTAL_YIELD — Lifetime cumulative yield. Not a predictive feature; encodes historical information.
  • SOURCE_KEY — 44 individual inverters. Too many for one-hot encoding; not needed for a general prediction form.
  • SENSOR_KEY — Only 2 values, redundant with PLANT_ID.
  • DATE_TIME — Night/day cycle already captured by IRRADIATION (zero irradiation at night → zero power).
